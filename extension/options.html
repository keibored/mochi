mochi ♡ extension settings

Adjust website categories

Automatic categories are ready without setup. They estimate a website's usual purpose, not your intent. Unknown and mixed-purpose websites record Neutral time. Rest is intentional and is never inferred from idle time.

Overrides are optional and take priority over automatic categories. Removing an override restores the automatic category or Neutral fallback. Your overrides match exact hostnames only: example.com does not include www.example.com. The bundled catalog includes subdomains only where explicitly configured.

These overrides belong only to this extension and will not be overwritten by web-app rules. An explicitly approved local dashboard receives category/time summaries, not these rules. Changes apply to future time only; saved activities and rewards are unchanged.

Only the hostname is saved. Paths, queries, fragments and URL ports are discarded; login details and unsupported protocols are rejected.

    No browsing history is stored. Local category aggregates are retained for up to seven days, with a maximum of 64 summaries and 200 explicit rules. No backend, accounts, analytics or network sync.
